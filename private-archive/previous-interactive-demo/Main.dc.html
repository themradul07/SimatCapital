DOWNSIDE RISK

Every fund falls sometimes. Our response is decided in advance.

OUR PLAN, DECIDED IN ADVANCE IN THE MANDATE
1
No panic selling

Positions are sized from the start so no single mistake or temporary market dislocation forces an exit.

2
Gold and cash cushion the fall

Mandatory defensive ballast (gold and unencumbered cash) is held directly in the portfolio to absorb volatility and protect capital.

3
Buy quality companies at lower prices

Cash provides immediate dry powder to systematically accumulate exceptional, pre-researched businesses when market prices fall below fair value.

THE HONEST PART

Shariah funds cannot hedge with derivatives. When markets fall, equity prices fluctuate. What we control is what we own, what price we paid, and having the ballast to endure drawdowns without becoming forced sellers.

FIDUCIARY MANDATE

Strict position caps (maximum 10% in any single equity) and pre-trade liquidity filters ensure risk budgets cannot be overridden in volatile markets.

WHY WE EXIST

Compliant is not the same as a good investment.

Buying a share takes seconds. Knowing whether it’s genuinely Shariah-compliant, well understood and worth the price takes far longer. Simat Capital was founded to close that gap.
OUR PHILOSOPHY

We ask better questions.

Markets can look chaotic, but price moves often tell a deeper story. When something changes, we don’t just react. We ask: Why now? What changed? Has this happened before?

Every investment then has to answer five questions
Select a question to see an illustrative answer.

And behind every decision sits one question: how does this benefit our investors?
OUR OBJECTIVE: ALPHA

We aim to beat the market, within Shariah limits.

Our objective is to outperform our benchmark on a risk-adjusted basis over a full market cycle. It’s an objective, not a promise, and our record is being built transparently.

Where we expect it to come from
Research beyond the numbers. Understanding management, positioning and threats, not just the financial statements.
Value discipline. Paying a sensible price, and holding cash when good ideas are scarce.
Overlooked opportunities. Compliant small and mid-sized companies that others under-research.
Speed on compliance. Re-screening as soon as new results come out, not at the next index review.
OUR STRATEGIES

Two strategies, one disciplined process.

Flagship

A focused portfolio built on conviction, not index weight. Hard position and sector limits mean no single idea can grow too large.

Catalyst

A separate strategy within the fund. Risk is set before every trade, and every trade has a stop.

Both are Shariah-screened using the MSCI Islamic method, and every decision is made and approved by a person. The rules are described here in principle; the demo that follows shows how they work.
